Privacy Policy

Brisbane Kerbside Map — Last updated: January 2025

Brisbane Kerbside Map does not collect, store on remote servers, or share any personal data. Your privacy is fully protected.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how the Brisbane Kerbside Map application ("the App", "we", "our") handles information when you use our service. The App is a standalone Progressive Web App (PWA) that operates primarily offline using bundled data.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect:

  • Names, email addresses, or any contact information.
  • Device identifiers, IP addresses, or location data.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports sent to third parties, or behavioural data.
  • Payment details (purchases are handled entirely by the Microsoft Store).

3. Local Storage (Device Only)

The App may use your browser's localStorage API to remember user preferences such as:

  • Last-viewed suburb or map position.
  • Display settings (e.g. colour-blind mode, legend visibility).

This data is stored exclusively on your device and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear this data at any time by clearing your browser or app storage (Settings → Apps → Brisbane Kerbside Map → Clear Data on most platforms).

4. Service Worker & Offline Caching

The App installs a Service Worker that caches application files locally on your device to enable offline use. Cached files include HTML, JavaScript, map data, and icons. This cache contains no personal information and is stored entirely on your device.

5. Network Requests

The App may make occasional network requests solely to check for updated collection-schedule data from Brisbane City Council's publicly available open data portal (data.brisbane.qld.gov.au).

These requests:

  • Do not include any personal or device-identifying information.
  • Are equivalent to any public web browser fetching a public URL.
  • Are not used for advertising, tracking, or profiling.

No third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking scripts are included in the App.

6. Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or social media services. Map tile rendering (if used) is performed locally using bundled boundary data — no external tile server requests are made.

7. Children's Privacy

This App does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). As the App collects no personal data from anyone, it is safe for all ages.

8. Data Security

Because no personal data is collected or transmitted, there is no personal data at risk. Application files cached locally on your device are protected by your operating system's standard security mechanisms.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
